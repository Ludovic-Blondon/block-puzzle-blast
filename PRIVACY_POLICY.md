# Privacy Policy

**Block Puzzle Blast**
Last updated: March 29, 2026

## Overview

Block Puzzle Blast is a puzzle game that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal information.** The app operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers.

## Data Storage

All game data (scores, achievements, settings, and progress) is stored locally on your device using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

Block Puzzle Blast does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Permissions

The app requests the following device capabilities:
- **Audio**: To play sound effects during gameplay.
- **Haptics**: To provide tactile feedback during interactions.

No other device permissions are requested or required.

## Children's Privacy

Block Puzzle Blast does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no social features.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us by opening an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Ludovic-Blondon/block-puzzle-blast).
